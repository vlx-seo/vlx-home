FAQ

Home inspection software questions

Does it work with no signal?
Yes. The offline inspection app runs in full offline mode — capture photos, notes and signatures with no signal, and everything syncs automatically when you reconnect.
Which inspection types does it support?
Full home inspections plus ancillary services — wind mitigation, 4-point, radon, mold, roof, sewer scope, pool and termite/WDO — each with its own template, pricing and state-required report forms.
Can I keep my report format?
Yes. VLX Home replicates your existing inspection report format — your branding, sections and state-required forms — so the deliverable still looks like yours.
How long does migration take?
Most inspectors switch in about an hour. Template migration is included: your templates, contacts, services and past inspections come with you.
Does it work for a solo inspector?
Yes. There's a plan for solo inspectors — home inspector software and a mobile home inspection app that fit pre-purchase inspections and insurance inspection checklists, whether you're one inspector or a growing team.
