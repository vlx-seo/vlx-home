How VLX Home Works

From the first booking to the final review

Four steps, mostly on autopilot.

1

Get booked

Drop one snippet on your website and it becomes a booking engine. Clients pick a service, see live availability and an instant quote, sign the agreement, and pay — no phone call.

Every booking lands as a confirmed job. VLX Home assigns the right inspector by proximity and availability, with conflict-free scheduling in the correct time zone.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything inspectors ask before switching to VLX Home.

What is home inspection software?
Home inspection software runs the business side of inspecting: online booking, scheduling and dispatch, agreements, reports, payments and a client portal. VLX Home does all of it in one platform, wrapped around the VLX field app.
What's included in VLX Home?
Online booking on your own site, scheduling and dispatch, AI-assisted reporting, 50-state compliance forms, Stripe payments and invoicing, a client portal, CRM and analytics — one login.
Does it support Wind Mitigation and 4-Point inspections?
Yes. VLX Home ships Full Home, Wind Mitigation and 4-Point templates, plus a 50-state compliance form registry including FL OIR-B1-1802, TREC REI 7-6 and Citizens 4-Point intake.
Can clients book inspections online?
Yes. Add a branded booking page or embed a widget on your existing site; clients pick a service, see a live quote and availability, sign the agreement and pay — no phone call.
Can I take payments and send invoices?
Yes. Collect deposits or full payment at booking via Stripe (card and ACH), auto-generate invoices on delivery, and sync to QuickBooks.
Does it integrate with Google Calendar and QuickBooks?
Yes — two-way Google Calendar sync and one-way QuickBooks sync, plus a REST API and webhooks for the rest of your stack.
Can inspectors write reports on-site?
Yes. Capture photos, video and voice notes in the field app; AI drafts the summary and defect narratives; you review and deliver a branded report to the client portal, often the same day.
How is VLX Home different from Spectora or ISN?
Most tools solve one slice — reports, or scheduling, or payments. VLX Home runs the whole path from online booking to payment in one platform, and switching takes about an hour.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Per-organization encryption (AES-256-GCM), role-based access enforced on the server, and a platform built and tested by the team behind VLX.
